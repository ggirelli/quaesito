# Code of conduct

Quaesito is about quizzes among friends, and this project should feel the same way: welcoming,
patient and fun, whoever you are and however much experience you have.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique ideas, not people.
- Help newcomers find their way.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/ggirelli/quaesito/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting them on GitHub. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban
contributors, for behaviour they deem harmful.
