# Testing a quiz

Press **🧪 Test** on the admin page, or **🧪 Test quiz** in the editor (which saves first), to play
the quiz on your own: the real big screen and two phones appear side by side, already joined.

![Test mode with the big screen and two phones](screenshots/test-mode.webp)

- **🧪 Test from here**, on any question or slide in the editor, skips the lobby and starts the
  test right there, to try the end of a long quiz without playing it all.
- **＋ Phone** adds a phone (up to six), ✕ removes one.
- **↺ Restart** starts over with a fresh game; **Edit quiz** goes back to the editor; **End test**
  closes it.
- Click the big screen once to use its keyboard shortcuts and hear its sounds.
- The QR code works too: join from a real phone to see the quiz in your hand.

Test games are never saved; one left open shows as "🧪 test" under **Live games**, where you can
end it.
