# Quiz settings

Each quiz has its own settings, at the top of the editor.

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Default time per question | 5–300 seconds; each question can override it |
| Reveal answers | after each question, with the standings; or at the end, walking through the answers before the podium (and at any [results slide](building-a-quiz.md#questions-and-slides) on the way) |
| Several correct answers | all or nothing, or partial credit (right picks minus wrong picks) |
| Points | speed (1000 when instant, down to 500 at the buzzer; answer times are shown to the millisecond) or 1000 for every right answer |
| Jokers | off, or each team may double one question's points, chosen while answering |
| Moving on | the host clicks, or the quiz continues by itself after a short pause |
| On team phones | coloured buttons only (read on the big screen), or the full question and answers |

Ties in the final ranking go to the team that spent less time on its right answers.
