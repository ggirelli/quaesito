[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-13

Pairs questions, a documented quiz file format, and progress bars for big imports and exports.

### Added

- Pairs questions: teams match each item on the left with its partner on the right (texts, pictures or both, two to six
  pairs). Phones show both columns, the right one shuffled; each pair matched earns its share of the points, and teams
  may leave some unmatched.
- A [quiz file format](https://ggirelli.github.io/quaesito/quiz-file-format/) page in the guide, with a JSON Schema
  for `quiz.json`: editors such as VS Code check quizzes written or generated by hand, and suggest field names. Exports
  name their format version and point to the schema.

### Changed

- Importing a quiz made with a newer version of Quaesito asks first, and lists the questions of kinds this version does
  not know: **Import without them** brings in the rest, instead of the whole import failing. Quaesito 0.3 and earlier
  still refuse such quizzes outright.
- Importing and exporting show their progress in a bar above the quiz list: the upload, the pictures and soundtracks
  being processed (12 of 80…), then the download of an export. Big quizzes no longer look stuck.
- Imported pictures are processed on every core at once, so big bundles import faster.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-11

A light theme, answers shuffled on every phone, and a Windows download that runs on a fresh computer.

### Added

- A light theme, next to the dark one: the sun and moon button on the home page, the admin pages and the big screen
  switches it, and every page open on that device follows.
- A "Shuffle answers" button in the quiz editor, next to "Shuffle questions": it shuffles the answers of every
  multiple-choice question (the right ones stay right), and can be undone the same way.
- Each phone lists the answers of a multiple-choice question in its own order, with the same shapes and colours as the
  big screen, so a glance at the next table's phone gives nothing away.
- "Shuffle the answers" when starting a live game: the answers of every multiple-choice question come in a new order for
  that game only, and the saved quiz keeps its own.

### Changed

- When faster answers earn more points, the big screen shows answer times to the millisecond (2.347 s), in the
  standings and next to the fastest team; with flat points they stay at a tenth of a second.
- The quiz editor has two sidebars that stay in view while you scroll: the buttons to add questions and slides on the
  left of the list, the shuffle buttons on its right. On narrow screens they stay below the list.

### Fixed

- The Windows build starts on computers without the Visual C++ Redistributable installed, such as a fresh Windows
  or a virtual machine, instead of stopping with "VCRUNTIME140.dll was not found".
- Leaving a test returns to the quiz list: "Back to admin" on the test's big screen ends the test instead of opening
  the admin pages inside it, and "End test" no longer stays on the test page.
- The admin login page has the home page's corner links: theme, user guide and home.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-11

More ways to ask questions, results at half time, and a quiz night that needs no internet at all.

### Added

- **Results slides**, for quizzes that reveal the answers at the end: placed anywhere (at an intermission, say), they
  walk through the answers since the previous results slide, then show the standings with the points each team
  gained and the places it moved.
- **Bonus questions** on choices, true/false, number, order and pin questions: a typed question answered together
  with the main one, worth +25%, +50% or +100% of its points and judged like typed answers on the reveal.
- **Text shown with a question**, line breaks kept: lyrics, a quote, a riddle.
- **Number ranges:** a number question can limit the guesses to a range; phones then show a slider next to the field
  for an exact guess.
- **Team limit:** starting a game asks how many teams may join, 50 by default, from 1 up to 10,000. The lobby shows
  the limit.
- **Test from here** on every question and slide in the editor starts a test right there, skipping the lobby.
- **Quiz list search and sorting** by last edit, newest, name or number of questions. **New quiz** sits next to
  Import and asks for the title in a pop-up.
- A demo quiz with one question of each kind, added the first time quaesito starts.
- A help button on the home page and in the admin pages, opening the user guide. The downloads include the guide,
  served by quaesito at `/docs/`, so it works offline.
- The README, the user guide and the downloads say up front that quaesito is free, MIT-licensed, without warranty and
  fully vibe coded, and that donations are welcome on Ko-fi.

### Changed

- Answers have new colours (violet, teal, magenta, orange, sky, lime) and shapes (sparkle, moon, heart, bolt, leaf,
  drop), drawn so they look the same on every phone. The right answer is ringed in white. The lobby illustration,
  the social preview, the screenshots and the demo video show them.
- Reveals say "Nobody answered this one." when no team answered, also in the walk through the answers at the end.
- Stopping the clock by hand stops the soundtrack too; when time runs out, the music still plays on through the
  reveal.
- Getting started walks through each system step by step, with the current macOS 15 "Open Anyway" advice and the
  Windows SmartScreen and firewall prompts.
- The README is shorter and points to the user guide for details.
- The demo video is narrated with Chatterbox, which sounds livelier than Kokoro, and shows a question with a picture
  and one with a soundtrack.

### Fixed

- Nothing is loaded from the internet any more: the user guide bundles its fonts and no longer asks GitHub for the
  repository's stars, and the server tells browsers to load nothing from other sites.
- The user guide at `/docs/` is never shown from a stale browser copy after it is rebuilt or the app is updated.
- A question's picture no longer pushes the big screen's controls off the bottom of a 16:9 screen: the big screen is
  exactly one screen tall and the picture takes the room the question leaves.
- Moving a question or slide in the editor keeps it on screen and keeps the focus on it.
- The editor's number fields look like the others, and a range that leaves out the correct number is flagged right
  under them.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-11

The first release: everything needed for a quiz night with friends.

### Added

- **Quiz editor** in the browser with autosave, a "ready to play" check, import and export (a zip
  with pictures and soundtracks), duplicate, shuffle (round by round between slides) and moving
  items to the top or bottom.
- **Question types:** multiple choice (with picture answers), true/false, typed answers judged by
  the host (full, half or no points, bonus and malus), closest number, put in order, several typed
  answers in one question, and a pin on a picture scored by distance. Slides between questions.
- **Pictures and sound:** question pictures (optionally revealed by zooming out or tile by tile),
  soundtracks started by the host together with the clock, and synthesised sound effects.
- **Big screen:** join QR code and lobby, live answer counts, reveals with the fastest team,
  points, answer times and rank changes, a podium revealed from third to first, and printable or
  CSV results.
- **Phones:** join by QR code or game code, team name and picture, and a pad for every question
  type; reconnects by itself.
- **Hosting:** pause, extra time, going back, renaming and removing teams, automatic or manual
  advance, and games that survive a server restart.
- **Settings per quiz:** reveal after each question or at the end, all-or-nothing or partial
  credit, speed or flat points, jokers, per-question points multiplier, and what phones show.
- **Test mode:** the big screen and phones side by side to try a quiz on your own.
- **Videos** in place of a question's or slide's picture, and configurable upload limits.
- **Downloads** for Linux, macOS (Apple silicon) and Windows with a start script, a documentation
  site with screenshots and a narrated demo video.
